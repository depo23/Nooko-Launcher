# Changelog

Nooko Launcher shows the section for its own version in **Nooko Launcher settings → Updates** after an update.
After an update, a card on the home screen shows the first 6 entries. Put the 6 most interesting changes first. Put small fixes last, as plain lines.
The build stops if `versionName` has no section here.

Write every entry in ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English:

- Write one idea in each sentence. Use 20 words or fewer in each sentence.
- Use the active voice and the present tense.
- Write instructions as commands: "Select **Updates**", not "**Updates** can be selected".
- Use one word for one thing. Use the names that Nooko Launcher shows on the screen.
- Start each entry with "- ". Nooko Launcher shows each entry as one item. It removes `**` marks.

Put changes that are not released under `## Unreleased`. Rename that heading to the version number when you release.

## 1.0.6

- **Every screen speaks your language.** Italian, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Turkish and Arabic now have all the menus, labels, hints and pop-ups. The pages that open on your phone for the TMDB key and photo upload follow the TV language too.
- **The heart messages are translated.** Each language has its own ten messages.
- **Reddit wallpaper.** It shows one random picture a day from r/TVWallpapersSFW. The subreddit list is gone.
- **Clearer help text.** The screensaver help no longer mentions features that are gone.

## 1.0.5

- **White clock and icons over the TMDB picture.** In fullscreen, the clock, the date and the top icons are always white. The wallpaper behind the picture no longer changes them.
- **Smaller detail lines.** The year, rating, director and cast lines use a smaller font.
- **Load your ATV Launcher settings.** Install ATV Launcher 1.7.4 or newer. In Nooko Launcher, select **Backup**, then **Load settings**. Nooko Launcher asks ATV Launcher for your settings, so no file is needed.

## 1.0.4

- **Updates are found again.** The update check, the weather and the place search no longer use saved answers. Nooko Launcher always asks the web.

## 1.0.3

- **Load settings finds the ATV Launcher backup.** Nooko Launcher asks to read files on Android 8 to 12, then looks in the places ATV Launcher saved its backup.
- **Watch Next is hidden for now.** The Watch Next switch and its sign-in settings are removed from **TMDB trending**. The cards always show TMDB trending titles.

## 1.0.2

- **More to read on the heart card.** Ten messages now, and a new one shows each time you open the card.

## 1.0.1

- **Support Nooko.** Select the heart in the top bar. A card shows a large QR code for Ko-fi. Scan it with your phone.

## 1.0.0

- **Welcome to Nooko Launcher.** A calm home screen for Android TV and Google TV, with your apps on a glass shelf.
- **Film and series cards.** Trending titles show as cards or fullscreen, with the streaming services you picked.
- **Wallpapers.** Use your own photos, the Bing picture of the day, or pictures from Reddit by theme.
- **Backup.** **Save settings** keeps every setting you can change in one file.
